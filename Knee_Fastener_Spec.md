# Knee-pitch fastener specification

Each knee-pitch assembly uses the following fasteners:

| Location | Fastener | Quantity per knee |
| --- | --- | ---: |
| Servo disc, first side | M3 | 4 |
| Servo disc, opposite side | M3 | 4 |
| Knee-pitch side mounting holes, first side | M2 × 6 mm | 3 |
| Knee-pitch side mounting holes, opposite side | M2 × 6 mm | 3 |
| **Total** |  | **14** |

For four legs, this is 32 M3 disc screws and 24 M2 × 6 mm knee-pitch mounting screws, 56 fasteners in total.

The M2 × 6 mm length is user-tested for the printed assembly. Screws are not included in the present MuJoCo inertial mass model because their individual masses have not been measured.
