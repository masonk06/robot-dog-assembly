Both trays have a flat 3 mm base and 4 mm retaining frame, with 0.5 mm lateral clearance. Use M3 90-degree countersunk screws with head diameter at most 6.4 mm. Install the tray before the board. Seat heads flush or below the surface. Use insulating hook-and-loop tape to retain the PCB and separate solder joints from the screw heads. Connector positions and underside solder projection require physical test fit. Perfboard mounts at chassis Z=37 mm. Waveshare mounts at chassis Z=9 mm. STL and STEP files have their print base at Z=0.
