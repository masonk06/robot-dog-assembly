# RoboDog frame V3 — electronics packaging revision

This separate revision uses `cad/solid_sources/FRAME V2.step` as its baseline.
It does not replace the live unsaved Fusion assembly, change joint locations,
or modify servo calibration. Dimensions are millimetres.

## Files

- `RoboDog_Frame_V3_Assembly.step`: the three printable parts, assembled.
- `RoboDog_Frame_V3_Layout_Reference.step`: the same assembly plus named battery,
  Jetson, and Waveshare packaging reference volumes. Reference volumes are not
  printable parts or vendor CAD.
- `Frame_V3.step` / `.stl`: reinforced replacement frame, same 170 x 180 mm
  footprint and original four hip-roll mounting regions.
- `Jetson_Tray.step` / `.stl`: removable open upper tray, 124 x 106 x 3 mm.
- `Waveshare_Sled.step` / `.stl`: removable lower side sled with 37 x 28 mm
  mounting spacing for the Waveshare Bus Servo Adapter (A).
- `validation.json`: geometry validity, solid counts, protected mount-zone
  comparisons, and packaging-intersection checks generated by the builder.

## Layout

The user's 75 x 34 x 25 mm LiPo sits low at the frame's XY centre (15, 0).
The cradle has a 79 x 38 mm interior and low rounded walls. Use a soft pad and
two 15 mm straps through the paired floor slots; the design allows 0.8 mm for
the pad in its reference layout. Battery removal is vertical after removing
the upper tray. Fit-check the pack with its actual lead exit and any swelling
clearance before using it.

The Jetson tray sits at Z=37..40. The full NVIDIA developer-kit envelope is
103 x 90.5 x 34.77 mm and reaches Z=74.77, above the original 50 mm frame walls.
The top is open for the cooler; there are no tall walls in front of the board
edges. Added frame material stays below or outside this volume. Four M3
clearance holes attach the tray to frame posts; the frame posts use blind
2.6 mm pilot holes for appropriate plastic-fastening screws. Adjustable slots
on the tray offer mounting flexibility. The native Jetson hole pattern has
not been verified from vendor board CAD, so physical hole/feet alignment must
be checked before calling the tray a finished Jetson screw mount. Do not
drill the Jetson PCB to match the tray.

The Waveshare board sits beside the battery on a separate low sled. Four
2.2 mm pilot holes accept suitable M2 fasteners into printed bosses (the
vendor PCB holes are 2.5 mm). Select screw length so tips remain in the bosses
and do not touch components. A 17 mm high volume above the mounting plane is
reserved as a conservative component-height allowance; it is not vendor
3D geometry. The outside edge opens toward +Y for cable access. Two M3
clearance holes attach the sled to blind floor pilots.

Internal rounded ribs and triangular gussets tie the floor into the existing
end walls. Floor slots provide wire and strap access with radiused ends.
The two outer bands at |Y| >= 60 mm, which contain the four servo mounting
regions, are compared to the baseline using exact solid Boolean operations.
Any change to those bands fails generation. No change to the external
170 x 180 mm build-plate footprint is intended.

## What still needs a physical check

- Correct baseline: the source STEP predates unsaved edits in the live Fusion
  document. Compare the frame-only geometry before replacing it in that assembly.
- Plug bodies, strain relief, and wire bends at every selected orientation.
  Empty geometric corridors do not prove fit for all cable types.
- Hip-roll motion and screws with the actual calibrated leg poses. Static
  mount-region preservation does not establish full swept clearance.
- Printed screw fits, Jetson mounting pattern/base, underside storage devices,
  and battery lead orientation.
- Structural strength, layer orientation, and cooling under load. Extra ribs
  are reinforcement geometry; no FEA or temperature test has been performed.

## Vendor dimensions

- NVIDIA: [Jetson Orin Nano carrier specification, figures 4-1 and 4-2](https://developer.nvidia.com/downloads/assets/embedded/secure/jetson/orin_nano/docs/jetson_orin_nano_devkit_carrier_board_specification_sp.pdf).
  Use the full-kit envelope, not just the 100 x 79 mm carrier PCB.
- Waveshare: [Bus Servo Adapter (A) product and hole spacing](https://www.waveshare.com/product/modules/motors-servos/bus-servo-adapter-a.htm).
  PCB is 42 x 33 mm; hole pitch is 37 x 28 mm.

## Regenerate

Use the system Python with OCP, numpy, and matplotlib:

```powershell
python cad/frame_v3/build_frame_v3.py
```

The builder deliberately fails on disconnected/invalid solids, packaging
intersections, or changes to the protected servo-mount zones. Its STEP files
can be opened in Fusion, and each printable STL is in the same mm coordinates.
Lay the frame on its original floor, and lay the tray/sled flat in the slicer.
