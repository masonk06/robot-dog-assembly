Use M3 90-degree countersunk screws with heads no larger than 6.4 mm. Attach trays to existing chassis posts first. Heads must sit flush or below the top surface. Apply hook-and-loop tape to the flat surfaces. Use insulating spacers or a backing plate under exposed PCB solder joints. Pi case vents must remain clear. Tray locations match the latest chassis; the standalone Waveshare tray can also be taped elsewhere for a second adapter. Physical screw fit and connector access need a test fit.
